What DreamWave does

The essentials for understanding your nights: whether you snore, how long and how well you slept, and a calmer way to wake up.

Hear exactly when you snore

Put your iPhone on the nightstand and DreamWave listens for snoring using Apple's on-device sound classifier. In the morning, play back short clips to hear it for yourself.

  • Short snore clips you can play back and delete
  • Nightly snore count and a 7-day snore trend
  • An intensity timeline showing when in the night it happened

A Smart Alarm that waits for you to stir

Pick a wake time and a window of up to 30 minutes. DreamWave rings when you start to stir inside that window, instead of at one fixed minute.

  • Rings even with the silent switch on
  • Keeps ringing on the lock screen if iOS closes the app
  • Your choice of alarm sound

Questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. DreamWave works with just your iPhone on the nightstand: snore recording, sleep score, duration, efficiency and the Smart Alarm all work without a watch. If you wear an Apple Watch, DreamWave can also show your deep, REM, light and awake time, read from Apple Health.

Where should I put my iPhone at night?

On the nightstand rather than on the bed, within about an arm's length of your head, ideally plugged in. Turn on Do Not Disturb so notifications don't wake you, and make sure your alarm is still allowed to sound.

Are my snore recordings uploaded anywhere?

No. Snore detection and recording happen on your iPhone. DreamWave has no account, no servers and no analytics, and its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. You can delete clips whenever you like.

Will the Smart Alarm ring if my phone is on silent?

Yes. The Smart Alarm rings even with the silent switch on, and it keeps ringing on the lock screen if iOS closes the app overnight. You choose a wake time and a window of up to 30 minutes, and it rings when you start to stir.

Can DreamWave tell me if I have sleep apnea?

No. DreamWave is not a medical device and cannot diagnose sleep apnea or any other condition. If you snore loudly with pauses in breathing, gasping or choking, or you feel exhausted during the day, please talk to a doctor.
